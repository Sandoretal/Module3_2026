Tutorial 2

Tabular health records and biological time series

Two of the most common kinds of real-world data in clinical neuroscience: loading, cleaning and summarising Electronic Health Records with pandas, then turning ECG and smartwatch accelerometer recordings into features. The day ends with a short task that links sensor features to each patient's health record.
